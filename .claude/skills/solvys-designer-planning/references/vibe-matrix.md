# Solvys Vibe Matrix

Choose one dominant vibe. Secondary influence is allowed only for narrow details.

## Shared Solvys Foundation

- Base canvas: `#050402`
- Surface 1: `#0a0905`
- Surface 2: `#110f0a`
- Surface 3: `#151310`
- Accent: `#c79f4a`
- Text: `#f0ead6`
- Data mono: Space Mono, JetBrains Mono, or repo mono
- Icon rule: line icons only, 1.5-2px, currentColor
- Motion rule: state and attachment first, decoration last

## Fintheon Product Foundation

## Artsy

Use when the product needs an editorial, cultural, object-led, or art-directed
website/app direction.

- Primary tool: OpenDesign when available locally.
- Research: Awwwards first, then Refero styles, then TypeUI artistic variants.
- Palette: Gallery `#f3efe4`, Ink `#11100c`, Vermilion `#c64e2e`,
  Mineral `#53645b`, Graphite `#2d2f2d`, Line `#c9c0ae`.
- Font kit: Playfair Display or Cormorant for display, Space Grotesk or Inter
  for body, Space Mono for data.
- Media: real object/product imagery, generated bitmap hero, editorial crops,
  or carefully framed app captures.
- Avoid: agency cliches, rotated filler text, random collage, ornamental SVG,
  and illegible art direction.

## Tech SaaS

Use when the product needs a serious product marketing site, operational SaaS
surface, dashboard, or investor/customer-facing app.

- Primary critic: Taste Skill plus Solvys `Design.md`.
- Research: Awwwards for hero and section rhythm, Refero for SaaS references,
  Superdesign for variants, TypeUI only for narrow system inspiration.
- Palette: Fintheon/Solvys Gold plus contrast support: Aegean `#2f6f73`,
  Oxide `#9a4b35`, Graph `#6b7280`, Safe `#2d5a3d`, Risk `#7a3030`.
- Font kit: Space Grotesk, Doto, Space Mono, and readable digits.
- Media: real app screenshots, product mockups, captured workflows, subtle
  effects added to screenshots, never fake placeholder cards.
- Hero: big real product image or generated bitmap background. No split
  text/card hero when a product image should carry the first viewport.
- Avoid: purple-blue gradients, generic metric rows, floating card piles,
  fake dashboards, and empty hero claims.

## Motion Designed

Use when motion, video, scroll rhythm, and overlays are part of the product
idea, not decoration.

- Primary tool: OpenDesign or Superdesign planning.
- Required planning step: user and agent discuss the background model,
  overlay model, legibility strategy, reduced-motion behavior, and media budget.
- Palette: Night `#050402`, Film `#080808`, Overlay `#151310cc`,
  Caption `#f0ead6`, Kinetic Gold `#c79f4a`, Signal `#2d5a3d`,
  Risk `#7a3030`.
- Font kit: Space Grotesk for readable UI, Doto for system labels, Space Mono
  for telemetry/captions.
- Motion patterns:
  - Scroll-travel background: background evolves as the user moves through
    sections; overlay content moves for legibility.
  - Ambient motion background: video/canvas runs independently; pinned or
    local overlays preserve readability.
  - Section media chapters: each section owns a media state and transition.
- Avoid: scroll-jacking, parallax gimmicks, inaccessible video text, motion
  without pause/reduced-motion control, and blurred dark stock footage.
