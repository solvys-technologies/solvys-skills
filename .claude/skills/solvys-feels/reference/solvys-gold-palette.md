# Solvys palette pairs

Canonical Factory palettes. **Forest Chartreuse** is the default.

| Pair | Light accent | Dark accent | Light hex | Dark hex |
|------|--------------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| Forest Chartreuse | Sunlit Chartreuse | Deep Forest Green | `#DCD870` | `#223D22` |
| Sunrise Gold | Sunrise Gold | Deep Vine Green | `#EDB964` | `#3B3D26` |
| Misty Blue | Misty Blue | Midnight Teal | `#83A7B4` | `#062635` |
| Pale Harbor | Pale Mis | Deep Harbor | `#C0C8CA` | `#2B4851` |

## Surface rules

- Light canvases: nardo grey (`#C0C8CA` family), not white
- Dark canvases: `#1f1f1f` or `#5C5858` on black

## OKLCH (Forest Chartreuse)

| Token | Hex | OKLCH |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Light accent | `#DCD870` | `oklch(0.864 0.127 106.8)` |
| Dark accent | `#223D22` | `oklch(0.330 0.057 144.1)` |
| Light surface | `#C0C8CA` | `oklch(0.827 0.009 214.4)` |
| Dark surface | `#1f1f1f` | `oklch(0.239 0 0)` |

## Legacy gold

`#c79f4a` was the retired Factory gold accent. Do not use it for new default theming.
