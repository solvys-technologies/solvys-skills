# Solvys Spec Kit Constitution

work freely, work with an open mind, and explore all possible options; never jump to conclusions at the second or third blocker. Be innovative, take inventory of your skills

## Principles

1. Start from current repository, runtime, provider, account, and ownership truth.
2. Specify user-visible behavior before implementation details.
3. Resolve material ambiguity and use Given/When/Then acceptance.
4. Prefer existing seams, native platform capability, approved OSS, and small
   maintainable changes in that order.
5. Keep one Solvys Development Contract as implementation authority.
6. Verify each ordered task before starting the next task.
7. Stop a corrected path until its prevention test passes.
8. Keep test, runtime, provider, Site, deployment, installation, and human proof separate.
9. Preserve dirty work, protected zones, client partitions, secrets, and `main`.
10. Human gates are limited to identity, cost, rights, secret issuance, consent,
    MFA, CAPTCHA, client decisions, and irreversible actions.

## PL lifecycle

- PL0: constitution, outcome, scope, ambiguity, acceptance.
- PL1: source research, approved precedent, OSS, constraints.
- PL2: plan, architecture, tasks, ownership, proof, rollback.
- PL3: backend implementation and failure proof.
- PL4: interface and integration after process contracts pass.
- PL5: cross-artifact analysis, drift reconciliation, release, and handoff.
