# Solvys Spec Kit Constitution

work freely, work with an open mind, and explore all possible options; never jump to conclusions at the second or third blocker. Be innovative, take inventory of your skills

## Core principles

### I. Current truth first

Verify the repository, exact ref and SHA, runtime, provider, account, dirty owner,
protected zones, and proof rung before planning or implementation.

### II. One authorization envelope

Use Spec Kit to generate and reconcile planning artifacts. The approved Solvys
Development Contract remains the only implementation, authority, and proof
envelope. Reject duplicate authorities.

### III. Behavior before implementation

Specify user outcomes, scope, edge cases, and Given/When/Then acceptance before
technical choices. Increment the Development Contract revision when requirements
or architecture change.

### IV. Small, proven delivery

Prefer accepted seams, approved OSS, and small ordered tasks. Verify each task at
its required reality rung before starting the next dependent task.

### V. Persistent exploration with hard safety gates

Inventory skills, current source, CLI, browser, provider API, approved OSS, and
materially different safe paths before a handoff. Preserve secret, cost,
destructive-action, ownership, client, provider, and proof gates.

## PL lifecycle

- PL0 owns the constitution, problem, user outcomes, scope, ambiguities, and acceptance.
- PL1 owns current-source research, accepted precedent, OSS, licenses, constraints, and dependency decisions.
- PL2 owns architecture, ordered tasks, ownership, protected zones, exact proof commands, rollback, and the approved Development Contract.
- PL3 executes backend tasks against approved contracts and proves failures and rollback.
- PL4 executes interface and integration tasks after process and data contracts pass.
- PL5 runs cross-artifact analysis, drift checks, proof reconciliation, and handoff.

## Governance

TP owns product and client decisions. Agents may branch, commit, open pull
requests, review, and collect proof inside approved scope. Merge, deployment,
provider mutation, secrets, cost, and destructive actions remain separately
gated. Amend this constitution through a reviewed revision and re-run the
Spec Kit to Development Contract mapper before implementation resumes.
